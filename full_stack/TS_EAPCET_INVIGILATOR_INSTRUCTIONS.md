# TS EAPCET Mock Exam Invigilator Instruction Sheet

This sheet is for invigilators, lab assistants, teachers, and coordinators who help students take the mock exam smoothly.

## Purpose

Use this guide to help students:

- open the mock exam correctly
- enter the required details before starting
- understand the paper password for each mock paper
- navigate the exam interface
- submit successfully
- access the solution sheet after submission

## Who is this for?

This guide is intended for:

- school staff
- junior college staff
- coaching centre staff
- computer lab coordinators
- volunteers helping Inter 2nd Year / Class 12 students

## Exam format overview

The mock exam follows the TS EAPCET engineering pattern:

- Duration: 180 minutes
- Total questions: 160
- Mathematics: 80 questions
- Physics: 40 questions
- Chemistry: 40 questions
- Objective type
- 1 mark for each correct answer
- No negative marking

## Before students arrive

Do the following checks before starting the session:

### Device readiness

- ensure each laptop/desktop is charged or connected to power
- ensure keyboard and mouse are working
- ensure the browser opens properly
- close unnecessary tabs and apps

### Internet readiness

- confirm internet is stable
- open the exam website once before students sit down
- verify the page loads fully

### Browser readiness

- use a modern browser such as Chrome, Edge, or Firefox
- clear very old cached data if the page looks outdated
- avoid browser extensions that block scripts or pop-ups

### Exam information to keep ready

- the website URL
- the mock paper number to be attempted
- the correct paper password
- rough start time and expected end time

## What students need before starting

Each student should know:

- their own full name
- which mock paper they are taking
- the correct password for that paper

## Student check-in steps

Ask each student to:

1. Open the exam homepage.
2. Click **Start mock paper** for the assigned paper.
3. Enter:
   - their full name
   - the mock paper password
4. Click **Continue to mock paper**.

## Invigilator script for starting the exam

You can read this aloud:

> You are about to begin the mock exam. Please check that you selected the correct paper. Enter your full name exactly as instructed. Use the password given for your paper. Once the exam starts, answer carefully, use the palette to move between questions, and submit only when you are ready.

## What students will see during the exam

On the live exam screen, students will see:

- mock paper title
- subject of the current question
- question number
- timer
- answered count
- marked count
- total questions
- question palette
- answer options
- submit button

## How to guide students during the exam

### Answering questions

Tell students:

- click one option to answer a question
- use **Next** and **Previous** to move
- use **Clear response** if they want to remove an answer
- use **Mark for review** if they want to come back later

### Question palette

Explain that:

- each number is a question
- clicking a number jumps directly to that question
- the palette helps them quickly review unanswered or marked questions

### Time management

Advise students to:

- not spend too long on one difficult question
- first answer easy and moderate questions
- review marked questions later

## What invigilators should not do

- do not tell students the answers
- do not change answers on behalf of students
- do not share the wrong mock paper password
- do not ask students to refresh the page unless necessary
- do not close the browser during an active attempt

## What to do if a student enters the wrong password

If a student cannot start the paper:

1. confirm the correct mock paper number
2. confirm the correct password from the password list
3. check for typing mistakes such as:
   - wrong number
   - capital letters
   - extra spaces
4. ask the student to try again

Example:

- for Mock Paper 4, the password must be `ats4`

## What to do if the student name is missing

If the exam does not start:

- ask the student to enter their full name in the name field
- avoid short placeholders like only initials if you need student records later

## What to do if the page is slow

Try these quick fixes:

1. wait a few seconds
2. check internet connectivity
3. close background tabs or heavy apps
4. verify that many students are not downloading large files at the same time
5. if needed, reopen the homepage and restart carefully

## What to do if the page does not load correctly

Try:

1. refresh the page once
2. open the site in another browser tab
3. clear cache if the design looks broken or outdated
4. check whether the internet is available

## What to do if the timer is already running but the student is confused

Tell the student:

- do not panic
- use the question palette
- continue from the current question
- focus on answering known questions first

## What to do if the student wants to leave the exam

Before they click **Leave exam**, confirm that they understand:

- leaving the exam will stop the current attempt flow
- they may lose progress if they exit carelessly

Ask them to continue unless there is a real issue.

## What happens after submission

After clicking **Submit paper**:

- the score is shown
- subject-wise summary is shown
- detailed solution sheet appears on the page
- the student's name is shown in the results view
- the student can download the solution sheet as a PDF

## Recommended post-exam steps for students

After submission, ask students to:

1. review incorrect answers
2. note weak areas by subject
3. download the PDF solution sheet
4. use the explanation notes to revise

## If the solution email option is unavailable or unreliable

Use this fallback:

- ask the student to click **Download solution sheet PDF**

This is the fastest and most reliable method for taking the result away.

## Suggested invigilator checklist

Use this quick checklist during the session:

- [ ] correct website opened
- [ ] correct mock paper selected
- [ ] student entered full name
- [ ] correct paper password entered
- [ ] exam started successfully
- [ ] timer visible
- [ ] student understands navigation
- [ ] student submitted successfully
- [ ] student reviewed or downloaded the PDF solution sheet

## Suggested instructions to read before the exam starts

> Please make sure you are opening the correct mock paper. Enter your full name carefully. Use the password assigned for your paper. The exam contains 160 questions and lasts 180 minutes. There is no negative marking. You may mark questions for review and return to them later. Submit only after checking your answers.

## Suggested instructions to read after the exam ends

> Your score and explanations are now visible. Please review your mistakes carefully. Download the solution sheet PDF before closing the page so you can revise later.

## Contact/coordination note for institutions

If you conduct the mock exam in a school or college lab, keep one coordinator responsible for:

- paper assignment
- password distribution
- browser/device support
- result/PDF download support

This reduces confusion and helps students focus on the exam.
